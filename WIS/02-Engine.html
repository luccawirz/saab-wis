Checks

  1. Remove the turbo heat shield by undoing the bolts and pulling the shield straight up out of the clip on the back.

  2. Detach the hose from the diaphragm box.

  3. Remove the control arm retaining clip with 83 94 538 Circlip tool.

  14. Refit the hose to the diaphragm box.

Diese WIS-Prozedur beschreibt Coolant freezing point and level am Saab 9-3 (9440, MY2005). Vor Beginn Fahrzeug sicher abstützen, Batterie-/Hydraulik-/Kühlsystem-Hinweise der jeweiligen Baugruppe beachten und markierte Bauteile beim Ausbau sauber wieder zuordnen. Drehmomente, Spezialwerkzeuge und Warnhinweise sind unten direkt in den Arbeitsschritten hervorgehoben.

Hinweis: The cooling system is under pressure. Hot coolant and steam can escape.

Hinweis: - Open the cap slowly to release the pressure.

Hinweis: - Carelessness can cause eye and burn injuries

Arbeitsschritte

SchrittBeschreibung
1Remove the expansion tank cap.
2Use 32 025 077 Fluid tester to check the coolant’s freezing point. Adjust as necessary using 40 - 93 170 402 Coolant (the coolant is blended - equal parts coolant and water).
3Check the coolant level. Adjust as necessary to the correct level.
4Fit the expansion tank cap.

Test pressurising the cooling system

Diese WIS-Prozedur beschreibt Test pressurising the cooling system am Saab 9-3 (9440, MY2005). Vor Beginn Fahrzeug sicher abstützen, Batterie-/Hydraulik-/Kühlsystem-Hinweise der jeweiligen Baugruppe beachten und markierte Bauteile beim Ausbau sauber wieder zuordnen. Drehmomente, Spezialwerkzeuge und Warnhinweise sind unten direkt in den Arbeitsschritten hervorgehoben.

Hinweis: The cooling system is under pressure. Hot coolant and steam can escape.

Hinweis: - Open the cap slowly to release the pressure.

Hinweis: - Carelessness can cause eye and burn injuries

Arbeitsschritte

SchrittBeschreibung
1Remove the cap on the expansion tank.
2Fit 83 96 160 Adapter, cooling system tester to the expansion tank.
3Fit 30 05 469 Adapter, cooling system tester to adapter 83 96 160.
4Connect 30 05 477 Cooling system tester .
5Pump a pressure of approx. 1 bar.
6Check that the pressure in the system does not drop.
7To find any leaks in the cooling system, continue to pressurise it while inspecting for leaks.
8Release the pressure and remove the tools.
9Fit the cap on the expansion tank.

1Raise the radiator, making sure its upper rubber pads end up in their mountings. Fit the lower radiator support against the subframe. Tightening torque 47 Nm (35 lbf ft)
2Check that the radiator and intercooler are connected. Important Make sure the lower catches of the fan cowling go into their mountings. Otherwise, there is a risk that the cooling system could be damaged.
3Check that the radiator and fan cowling are connected.
4Remove the straps.
5Aut: Fit the lower radiator connection between the gearbox and radiator.
6Attach the lower radiator hose between the radiator and cylinder block.

12Fit the fan cowling.
13Attach the upper coolant hose between the radiator and cylinder block.
14Attach the radiator hose between the radiator and the expansion tank.
15Attach the pipe to the charge air cooler, but do not tighten yet. Attach the hose between the throttle body and pipe.
16Plug in the connector of the turbo pressure sensor.
17Attach the hose to the turbo pressure sensor.
18Raise the car.
19Fit the lower charge air pipe to the fan cowling.
20Attach the lower hose between the throttle body and charge air cooler.
21Fit the lower section of the spoiler.
22Lower the car.
23Tighten the bolt of the charge air pipe to the fan cowling.
24Fit the upper engine cover.
25Connect a 30 05 477 Cooling system tester , pump up a pressure of about 1 bar and check the cooling system to ensure the pressure does not drop.
26Relieve the pressure and remove the cooling system tester.
27Top up coolant. See Bleeding the cooling system .
28Fit the expansion tank cap.
29Fit the battery cooler pipe.
30Attach the battery cables to the batter and fit the battery cover.
31Aut: Check the automatic transmission fluid level.
