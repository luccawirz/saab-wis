The red visible light is of laser class 1. Do not look directly into the optic fibre or the control module connector at close range. At a distance of less than 20 mm from your eyes to the light source, your eyes could be damaged.

It is not uncommon and perfectly normal for the value of a counter in a car that has been driven large distances to be 255.

The counters are very sensitive and generally show 255, even after a short period of time. Starting the engine and the activation of the O-bus when the radio is turned on usually cause the value of the counters to increase a couple of digits.
