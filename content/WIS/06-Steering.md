## Power Steering System — Adjustment/replacement Procedures


### Power steering pump, B207 petrol engine

> 🔧 [Online öffnen](https://saabwisonline.com/9-3-9440/2005/6-steering-assembly/power-steering-system/adjustment-replacement/power-steering-pump-b207-petrol-engine/)

#### Power steering pump, B207 petrol engine
⚠️ **Important**
| Scrupulous cleanliness must always be observed during all work with hydraulic components.
|
#### To remove

1. Detach the return hose, wipe up any fluid spill with a cloth and plug the reservoir pipe using 82 92 955 Plugs, spare part kit . Suspend the hose so that fluid does not run out.

2. Remove the electrical connection of the pressure sensor.

3. Remove the delivery pipe from the pump. Wipe up any oil spill with a cloth.

4. Remove the pump. Some engine oil may run out.

5. Empty the fluid from the pump.

#### To fit

1. Fit the pump using a new gasket. **Tightening torque 22 Nm (16 lbf ft)**

2. Connect the return hose.

3. Connect the delivery line using new gaskets. **Tightening torque 32 Nm (24 lbf ft)**

4. Connect the electrical connection of the pressure sensor.

5. Fill with fluid and bleed the power steering system . Check for leaks.


### Delivery line for power steering, B207 LHD

> 🔧 [Online öffnen](https://saabwisonline.com/9-3-9440/2005/6-steering-assembly/power-steering-system/adjustment-replacement/delivery-line-for-power-steering-b207-lhd/)

#### Delivery line for power steering, B207 LHD
⚠️ **Important**
| Scrupulous cleanliness must always be observed during all work with hydraulic components.
|
⚠️ **Important**
| Always use wing covers when working in the engine bay.
|
#### To remove

1. Lift off the battery cover and disconnect the negative battery cable.

2. Clamp the return hose using 30 07 739 Hose pinch-off pliers .

3. Place a receptacle under the car and detach the delivery line from the steering gear.

4. Detach the power steering pipe from the power steering pump.

5. Undo the plastic clips on the delivery line.

6. Raise the car.

7. Remove the front right wheel.

8. Remove the right-hand wheel arch liner.

9. Remove the front lower cover.

10. Remove the front bumper shell.

11. Suspend the radiator assembly from the radiator member with two 83 95 212 Straps .

12. Remove the right-hand air shield.

13. Remove the screw holding the front torque arm to the subframe.

14. Remove the brake hose clips and unhook the hose from the mounting on the suspension strut.

15. Remove the bolt from the steering swivel member. Lower the suspension arm and place a 83 95 238 Wedge between the suspension arm and anti-roll bar.

16. Tap out the drive shaft from the intermediate shaft using a brass drift and mallet. Move aside the drive shaft.

17. Place a column jack under the subframe and remove the front bolts.

18. Open the plastic clip on the subframe and remove the cooling circuit clamps.

19. Lower the front edge of the subframe.

20. Remove the power steering pipe.

#### To fit

1. Fit the power steering pipe and cooling coil clamps.

2. Fit the pipe in the plastic clip outer hole.

3. Raise the subframe with the column jack and tighten the bolts. **Tightening torque 75 Nm** +135° (**55 lbf ft** +135°)

4. Remove the column jack. Fit the bolt holding the front torque arm to the subframe. **Tightening torque 60 Nm** +90° (**44 lbf ft** +90°)

5. Remove the straps.

6. Insert the drive shaft into the intermediate shaft. Make sure the lock ring clicks into place.

7. Fit the brake hose and the clip.

8. Remove the wedge and attach the ball joint to the steering swivel member with a nut and bolt. The pin must be visible above the steering swivel member before the bolt is fitted. Tighten the nut. ⚠️ Warning Press up the pin carefully. The groove in the pin must be visible in the screw hole in the spindle housing. If the rubber gaiter is pressed down, it will not seal properly to the swivel pin. **Tightening torque 50 Nm (37 lbf ft)**

9. Fit the right-hand air shield.

10. Fit the wheel arch liner.

11. Fit the front bumper shell.

12. Fit the front, lower engine cover.

13. Fit the wheel.

14. Lower the car to the floor.

15. Fit the power steering hose to the steering gear. **Tightening torque 28 Nm (21 lbf ft)**

16. Fit the power steering hose to the power steering pump using a new seal, and to the hose clip. **Tightening torque 32 Nm (24 lbf ft)**

17. Remove the hose pinch-off pliers.

18. Connect the negative battery cable and fit the battery cover.

19. Fill with power steering fluid, bleed the power steering system and check for leaks.

20. Perform the measures necessary for after disconnecting the battery.


### Power steering delivery line, B207 RHD

> 🔧 [Online öffnen](https://saabwisonline.com/9-3-9440/2005/6-steering-assembly/power-steering-system/adjustment-replacement/power-steering-delivery-line-b207-rhd/)

#### Power steering delivery line, B207 RHD
⚠️ **Important**
| Scrupulous cleanliness must always be observed during all work with hydraulic components.
|
⚠️ **Important**
| Always use wing covers when working in the engine bay.
|
#### To remove

1. Lock the steering wheel in the straight ahead position. Fix the steering wheel to the dashboard with woven tape or lock it with the steering wheel lock.

2. Lift off the battery cover and disconnect the negative battery cable.

3. Remove the pipes from the bracket on top of the steering gear. Leave the screw and clamp in place.

4. Clamp the return hose using 30 07 739 Hose pinch-off pliers .

5. Detach the power steering pipe from the power steering pump.

6. Undo the plastic clips on the delivery line.

7. Raise the car.

8. Remove the front right wheel.

9. Remove the right-hand wheel arch liner.

10. Remove the front lower cover.

11. Remove the front bumper shell. CV: Remove Chassis reinforcement, front subframe .

12. Suspend the radiator assembly from the radiator member with two 83 95 212 Straps .

13. Remove the right-hand air shield.

14. Cars with Xenon lamps: Remove the cable clips from the subframe.

15. Remove the steering shaft joint.

16. Remove the exhaust pipe flange and front exhaust pipe mountings. Relieve the load on the flex bellows using 83 95 212 Strap .

17. Remove the rear torque rod bolt.

18. Remove the screw holding the front torque arm to the subframe.

19. Remove the front part of the wheel arch liner and move it aside slightly.

20. Position 83 95 311 Trolley lift with 83 94 801 Parent fixture and 83 96 137 Centring tool, subframe - body .

21. Remove the subframe bolts on both sides and remove the rod from the body.

22. Lower the subframe slightly (about 100 mm).

23. Detach the delivery line from the steering gear.

24. Open the plastic clips on the subframe.

25. Remove the cooling circuit clamps and the power steering pipe.

#### To fit

1. Fit the power steering pipe and cooling coil clamps.

2. Fit the pipe in the plastic clips.

3. Replace the O-ring and fit the delivery pipe to the steering gear. check that the pipe sits in the mounting on the left side of the steering gear. **Tightening torque 30 Nm (22 lbf ft)**

4. Raise the subframe using 83 95 311 Trolley lift and fit 83 96 137 Centring tool, subframe - body at the guide holes in the body. Fit the rod and the bolts. Remove the trolley lift. **Tightening torque 75 Nm** +135° (**55 lbf ft** +135°)** **Tightening torque, rear rod: **90 Nm** +45° (**66 lbf ft** +45°)

5. Fit the front part of the wheel arch liner.

6. Put the wheels in the straight ahead position and fit the steering shaft joint to the steering gear. Clean the threads and apply Threadlock, Loctite 242 to the threads. **Tightening torque 30 Nm (22 lbf ft)** ⚠️ Warning Check that the bolt fits into the groove on the pinion shaft.

7. Fit the screw holding the front torque arm to the subframe. **Tightening torque 60 Nm** +90° (**44 lbf ft** +90°)

8. Fit the rear torque rod. **Tightening torque: **80 Nm** (**59 lbf ft**)

9. Remove the straps.

10. Fit the exhaust pipe flange and the exhaust pipe mountings. **Tightening torque 30 Nm (22 lbf ft)**

11. Cars with Xenon lamps: Fit the cable clips to the subframe.

12. Fit the right-hand air shield.

13. Fit the wheel arch liner.

14. Fit the front bumper shell. CV: Fit Chassis reinforcement, front subframe .

15. Fit the front, lower engine cover.

16. Fit the wheel.

17. Lower the car to the floor.

18. Fit the power steering hose to the power steering pump using a new seal, and to the hose clip. **Tightening torque 32 Nm (24 lbf ft)**

19. Remove the hose pinch-off pliers.

20. Connect the negative battery cable and fit the battery cover.

21. Tighten the clamp to the steering gear.

22. Fill with power steering fluid .

23. Bleed the power steering system and check for leaks.

24. Perform the measures necessary for after disconnecting the battery.

25. Check the position of the steering wheel when driving straight ahead on a level road. Adjust as necessary.

4. Attach the line to the power steering fluid reservoir.

5. Fill with fluid and bleed the power steering system .

6. Fit the wheel .


### Power steering return line, B207 RHD

> 🔧 [Online öffnen](https://saabwisonline.com/9-3-9440/2005/6-steering-assembly/power-steering-system/adjustment-replacement/power-steering-return-line-b207-rhd/)

#### Power steering return line, B207 RHD
⚠️ **Important**
| Scrupulous cleanliness must always be observed during all work with hydraulic components.
|
#### To remove

1. Remove the air filter cover with hose and remove the connectors.

2. Place a suitable receptacle under the steering gear.

3. Remove the pipe from the bracket on top of the steering gear. Leave the screw and clamp in place.

4. Disconnect the delivery line connection from the steering gear.

5. Disconnect the return line from the steering gear.

6. Remove the line from the reservoir.

7. Remove the line from the clasps.

8. Lift up the line.

#### To fit

1. Fit the line in place. Attach the return line to the steering gear. Make sure the O-ring is correctly seated. ⚠️ Important Make sure that the return line (line with the short nut) is connected to the lower of the two connections. The steering gear will be destroyed if the lines are mixed up! **Tightening torque 28 Nm (21 lbf ft)**

2. Attach the delivery line with a new O-ring to the steering gear. **Tightening torque 28 Nm (21 lbf ft)**

3. Fit the pipe to the bracket on top of the steering gear.

4. Fit the pipe and the hose clip to the power steering fluid reservoir.

5. Fit the hose into the clasps.

6. Fit the air filter cover with hose and plug in the connector.

7. Fill with fluid and bleed the power steering system . Check for leaks.


### Gaiter, steering gear

> 🔧 [Online öffnen](https://saabwisonline.com/9-3-9440/2005/6-steering-assembly/power-steering-system/adjustment-replacement/gaiter-steering-gear/)

#### Gaiter, steering gear
#### To remove

1. Raise the car.

2. Remove the wheel.

3. Slacken the locknut.

4. Remove the nut securing the track rod end to the steering swivel member.

5. Part the track rod end from the steering swivel member with 87 91 287 Puller, 150 mm .

6. Unscrew the track rod end from the track rod, counting the number of turns.

7. Blow the area clean with compressed air and remove the clips from the gaiter. Pull off the gaiter.

#### To fit

1. If necessary, carefully wipe the track rod clean and examine the ball joint. ⚠️ Important If sand, gravel or other contaminants enter the ball joint (e.g. if the boot has been defective for a time), the track rod must be changed.

2. Fit a new gaiter to the track rod and steering gear housing. Fit new clips using 30 16 235 Pliers to secure them.

3. Screw on the track rod end the same number of turns as when it was removed. ⚠️ Note The left and right track-rod ends are different. The track rod ends should curve slightly backwards when fitted. Markings: L = left-hand side, R = right-hand side.

4. Bolt the track rod end to the steering swivel member. **Tightening torque 35 Nm (26 lbf ft)**

5. Tighten the track rod end's locknut. **Tightening torque 70 Nm (55 lbf ft)**

6. Fit the wheel .

7. Lower the car to the floor.

8. Check the toe-in and adjust if required. For setting values, refer to Wheel geometry .


### Bleeding the power steering system

> 🔧 [Online öffnen](https://saabwisonline.com/9-3-9440/2005/6-steering-assembly/power-steering-system/adjustment-replacement/bleeding-the-power-steering-system/)

#### Bleeding the power steering system
1. Refill the system with new power steering fluid . ⚠️ Note Always use new fluid. Do not turn the steering wheel, as air can enter the steering gear requiring longer to purge.

2. Run the engine for 5 seconds. ⚠️ Important The pump can be damaged if the engine is run for more than 5 seconds with the fluid level too low.

3. Check the fluid level in the reservoir and top up with 93 160 548 Power steering fluid as necessary. At 20°C it should be between the MAX and MIN markings.

4. Start the engine and let it run until no more bubbles appear in the fluid reservoir. Use a torch to be able to see properly. Abnormal noise from the pump is an indication that there is still air in the system.

5. Switch off the engine and check the fluid level. Top up with 93 160 548 Power steering fluid as necessary.

12. Remove the tape or other means used to prevent the steering wheel from moving.

13. Bleed the power steering system and check for leaks.

14. Carry out a wheel alignment .

#### To remove

|

2. Remove the pipes from the bracket on top of the steering gear. Leave the screw and clamp in place.

3. Position the wheels and steering wheel straight ahead. Fix the steering wheel to the dashboard with woven tape or lock it with the steering wheel lock.

2. Remove the wheel.

3. Remove the hub nut.

4. Press in the drive shaft so that it loosens from the hub 89 96 951 Puller, hub drive shaft .

8. Remove the wheel sensor connection.

9. Remove the bolt securing the suspension arm ball joint to the steering swivel member.

10. Pull out the ball joint pin.

11. Place a wedge between the suspension arm and the anti-roll bar in order to hold down the suspension arm.

#### To fit

1. Clean the contact surfaces and fit the brake shield and the hub with three bolts. Make sure that the cable to the wheel sensor fits into position upwards/forwards. **Tightening torque 90 Nm** +45° (**66 lbf ft** +45°)

2. Insert the drive shaft into the hub. ⚠️ Important Always fit a new hub-centre nut if it has been removed because the clamping force of the lock indentations will be reduced if the old one is refitted.

3. Fit the hub nut and pull the shaft to the correct position with the nut.

4. Remove the wedge and fit the suspension arm to the steering swivel member. **Tightening torque 50 Nm (37 lbf ft)** ⚠️ Warning Check that the spindle on the ball joint protrudes above the steering swivel member attachment.

6. Connect the wheel sensor and fit the connector into the holder.

7. Fit the brake caliper. **Tightening torque 210 Nm** +30° (**155 lbf ft** +30°)

8. Fit the wheel .

9. Lower the car so that the wheel rests on the floor.

10. Tighten the hub nut. **Tightening torque 230 Nm (170 lbf ft)**

11. Fit the centre emblem.

12. Press out the piston in the brake caliper.

#### Metallic noise from front end/brakes


Symptom: Metallic noise from front end/brakes. The noise can be misinterpreted as a faulty/damaged wheel bearing.
The noise occurs during slow driving, when turning the steering wheel and, in some cases, when the brakes are applied lightly.
#### Conditions


    15”, 16” or 16"+ brake system.


#### Fault symptom


    Metallic noise from front end/brakes. The noise can be misinterpreted as a faulty/damaged wheel bearing.


    The noise occurs during slow driving, when turning the steering wheel and, in some cases, when the brakes are applied lightly.


    **Cause:**
    The brake anchor plate moves and scrapes against the steering swivel member.

#### Procedure

1. Slacken the wheel hub bolts (A).

2. Carefully use a rubber mallet to tap the top of the brake anchor plate (B) until it is in its bottom position.

3. Tighten the wheel hub bolts. **Tightening torque 90 Nm** +45° (**66 lbf ft** +45°)
