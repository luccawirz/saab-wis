Charging Systems — Adjustment/replacement Procedures

Generator, Z19

🔧 Online öffnen

Generator, Z19

To remove

  1. Remove the battery cover and disconnect the negative battery cable.

  2. DTH Remove the upper engine cover.

  3. DT: Remove the generator’s upper retaining bolt. DTH Remove the generator’s two upper retaining bolts.

  4. Raise the car.

  5. Remove the lower engine cover.

  6. Remove the front right wheel.

  7. Remove the right-hand Front wing liner .

  8. Release the load of the belt tensioner and lock it with 87 92 335 Lock pin .

  9. Lift the poly-v-belt off the alternator. ⚠️ Important Make a note of the belt’s direction of rotation. The belt must be refitted for the same direction of rotation as previously.

  10. Remove the bolt from the steering swivel member, lower the suspension arm and place a 83 95 238 Wedge between the suspension arm and the anti-roll bar.

  11. Tap the drive shaft out of the intermediate shaft with a brass drift and large hammer. Move aside the drive shaft. ⚠️ Warning Use protective goggles to protect against flying splinters of metal.

  12. Remove the generator’s electrical connections (ring terminals).

  13. DT: Remove the lower bracket. DTH: Remove the generator’s lower retaining bolt.

  14. Lower the alternator and lift it out.

To fit

**DT**


[/images/9-3-9440/img-f3/f321r129.svg](https://saabwisonline.com/images/9-3-9440/img-f3/f321r129.svg)

DTH

/images/9-3-9440/img-f3/f321r122.svg

  1. Lift the generator into place. DT: Fasten the lower bracket. DT/DTH: Insert the lower bolt.

  2. Lower the car.

  3. DT: Fit the generator’s upper bolt. DTH: Fit the generator’s upper bolts. Tightening torque 60 Nm (44.5 lbf ft)

  4. Raise the car.

  5. DT: Tighten the bolts for the lower bracket. Tightening torque M8: 25 Nm (18.5 lbf ft) M10: 50 Nm (37 lbf ft) DT/DTH: Tighten the lower bolt. Tightening torque 60 Nm (44.5 lbf ft)

  6. Connect the ring terminals to the generator. Tightening torque M5: 5 Nm (3.7 lbf ft) M8: 10 Nm (7.5 lbf ft)

  7. Grease the drive shaft splines with 90 513 210 Universal paste and make sure the rubber seal is mounted on the shaft.

  8. Fit the drive shaft to the intermediate shaft. Make sure the lock ring clicks into place.

  9. Remove the wedge and connect the ball joint to the steering swivel member with a nut and bolt. The pin must be visible above the steering swivel member before the bolt is fitted. Tighten the nut. ⚠️ Warning Press up the pin carefully. The groove in the pin must be visible in the screw hole in the spindle housing. If the rubber gaiter is pressed down, it will not seal properly to the swivel pin. Tightening torque 50 Nm (37 lbf ft)

  10. Refit the poly-V belt. ⚠️ Important Make a note of the belt’s direction of rotation. The belt must be refitted for the same direction of rotation as previously.

  11. Release the tension of the belt tensioner and remove 87 92 335 Lock pin . ⚠️ Note Make sure that the belt is correctly positioned on all the pulleys.

  12. Fit Front wing liner .

  13. Fit the lower engine cover.

  14. Fit the wheel.

  15. Lower the car.

  16. Connect the negative cable of the battery.

  17. Fit the battery cover.

  18. DTH: Fit the upper engine cover.

  19. Reset the car; see Procedures after battery disconnection .

Generator, B207

Generator, B207

To remove

  1. Remove the battery cover and disconnect the negative battery cable.

  2. Raise the car.

  3. Remove the lower engine cover.

  4. Place a receptacle under the car, connect a hose to the radiator and drain the coolant.

  5. Remove the plastic nuts from the wheel arch liner.

  6. Move aside the wheel arch liner, unload the belt tensioner and take off the belt (mark the belt direction). Use 83 96 095 Unloading tool, belt circuit B207E/L .

  7. Lower the car.

  8. Remove the upper engine cover.

  9. Unplug the connectors from the engine control module.

  10. Remove the engine control module.

  11. Detach the air hose from the air filter housing.

  12. Remove the cover of the air filter housing.

  13. Remove the air filter.

  14. Unplug the connector from the pressure sensor.

  15. Detach the intake air pipe from the air filter housing and lift up the filter housing.

  16. Detach the coolant hose from the engine and radiator. Bend it aside.

  17. Detach the vent hose from the air hose to the throttle body and loosen the fastener in the camshaft cover.

  18. Bend aside the vent hose.

  19. Detach the generator connections.

  20. Remove the generator retaining bolts.

  21. Lift up the generator; see illustration.

To fit

  1. Position the generator correctly; see illustration.

  2. Fit the generator retaining bolts. Tightening torque 24 Nm (18 lbf ft)

  3. Attach the generator connections.

  4. Fit the vent hose fastener to the camshaft cover.

  5. Fit the vent hose to the throttle body air hose.

  6. Fit the coolant hose to the engine and radiator.

  7. Fit the intake air pipe to the air filter housing.

  8. Plug in the connector to the pressure sensor.

  9. Put the air filter in place.

  10. Fit the air filter housing cover.

  11. Fit the air hose to the air filter housing.

  12. Fit the engine control module.

  13. Plug in the engine control module connectors.

  14. Fit the upper engine cover.

  15. Raise the car.

  16. Move aside the wheel arch liner and unload the belt tensioner. Use 83 96 095 Unloading tool, belt circuit B207E/L and fit the belt (be certain it is fitted in the correct direction)

  17. Fit the plastic nuts on the wheel arch liner.

  18. Fit the lower engine cover.

  19. Lower the car.

  20. Connect the negative cable of the battery.

  21. Fit the battery cover.

  22. Reset the car; see Procedures after battery disconnection .

  23. Top up with coolant and bleed the cooling system following Bleeding the cooling system .

  24. When changing: Check battery, see Battery check and charge battery if necessary, see “Charging the battery.”

  7. Undo the alternator’s bracket: - Unplug the wiring harness from the oil pressure switch (3) - Undo the nut (2) - Undo the bolt (1) - Undo the lower bolt (4)

  8. Lower the car.

  10. Remove the air filter housing (1): - Unplug the wiring harness from (2) heating element - mass air flow sensor - Detach the wiring harness (arrows) - Remove the engine vent hose (3) - Remove the intake hose (4)

  11. Remove the tank purge valve: - Unscrew the bolt (arrow) - Detach the line (2) - 83 95 261 Fuel line tool (1) - Unplug the wiring harness connector (3)

  12. Remove the alternator belt: - Unscrew the upper bolt - Unscrew the lower bolt - Remove the alternator’s wiring harness - Unscrew 2 nuts (1) ⚠️ Note: Remove the generator upwards with the generator’s attaching plate

  13. Remove the alternator bracket: - Undo the bolt

  1. Fit the alternator bracket: - Screw in the bolt ⚠️ Note: Do not tighten the bolt

  2. Fit the alternator: ⚠️ Note: Position the generator from above with the generator’s attaching plate - Fit the alternator’s wiring harness - Tighten the nut - Screw in the upper bolt

  3. Raise the car.

  4. Fasten the generator’s attaching plate **- Tighten the bolt - Tightening torque 35 Nm **- Tighten the nut - Tightening torque 35 Nm - Wiring harness connector to the oil pressure switch.

  5. Fit the multi-groove belt tensioner **- Tighten the bolt - Tightening torque 35 Nm

  6. Lower the car.

  7. Fasten the generator **- Tighten the bolt - Tightening torque 20 Nm

  8. Fit the tank purge valve - Tighten the bolt - Plug in the wiring - Plug in the wiring harness connector

  9. Fit the air filter housing - Fit the intake hose - Fit the engine vent hose - Plug in the wiring harness connector - Fasten the wiring harness

  10. Raise the car.

  11. Fit the multi-groove belt: ⚠️ Note: Note the direction of rotation and installation position - Tension the multi-groove belt tensioner - Use KM-913-A or a 15 mm articulated wrench - Insert KM-6130 or a 4.5 mm drift - Slacken the multi-groove belt tensioner - Use KM-913-A or a 15 mm articulated wrench - Remove KM-6130

  12. Fit the multi-groove belt’s guard: - Tighten the bolt

  13. Lower the car.

  14. Fit the protective engine cover: - Unscrew the oil filler port cap **- Tighten the bolt - Tightening torque 8 Nm - Screw on the oil filler port cap

Battery check

🔧 Online öffnen

Battery check

  1. Switch off all consumers and remove the key from the ignition switch. ⚠️ Note Certain diagnostic trouble codes can be generated if the battery is tested while the key is in the ignition switch.

  2. Remove the battery cover. ⚠️ Note Low battery electrolyte level is indicated by white showing through the indicator window. A sufficient electrolyte level is indicated by black showing through the indicator window.

  3. Check the electrolyte level. The battery cannot be topped up. If the electrolyte level is too low, the battery must be changed.

  4. Remove the cover from the Underhood Electrical Centre (UEC).

  5. Remove fuses 30 and 37. Wait at least 20 seconds before connecting the battery tester.

  6. Connect the red cable clip to the positive terminal (+). (Reversed polarity will not damage the analyser.)

  7. Connect the black cable clamp to the negative (-) terminal. Make sure the cable clamp makes as good contact as possible. Follow the instructions on the display of the battery analyser. ⚠️ Note Once both clamps are connected, the analyser starts automatically. For a detailed description of how to use the analyser, refer to the analyser’s instruction manual.

  8. Under “SELECT TEST”, select whether the battery is connected to the vehicle electrical system (“IN VEHICLE”) or not (“OUTSIDE OF VEHICLE”). Select (“IN VEHICLE”) as the battery is connected in the vehicle.

  9. Under “BATTERY TYPE”, select whether the battery is factory-fitted (“SAAB BATTERY”) or a replacement battery from another manufacturer (“NON-SAAB”).

  10. Confirm with “SELECT TEST BY SAE(A)” if the battery’s cold starting current is specified according to CCA (SAE) standard. For batteries specified according to DIN standard, select and confirm with “SELECT TEST BY DIN (A)”. ⚠️ Important CCA (SAE) is always applicable for Saab’s factory-fitted batteries The DIN standard can be selected when the analyser is used to test replacement batteries that are not specified according to SAE.

  11. Enter the cold starting current for the battery in question. For Saab factory-fitted batteries, this text is found in capital letters on a label affixed to the top of the battery. After selecting and confirming a value, the actual battery test will start automatically. “SELECT MODE BEFORE CHARGE” “SELECT MODE AFTER CHARGE” Under certain voltage and conductance conditions, the analyser will require information on whether the test is being performed before or after the battery has been charged in order to assess it correctly.

  12. If the analyser indicates the battery needs to be replaced within the warranty period, note the test code. This test code should be included on the warranty claim (more information is available under Warranty policy). ⚠️ Important Also check the replacement battery with the battery analyser before fitting it in the car. If the cold start current of the replacement battery is not specified in accordance with the SAE standard then measurement in accordance with the DIN standard can be selected.

  13. Fit fuses 30 and 37.

  14. Fit the cover of the Underhood Electrical Centre (UEC).

  15. Fit the battery cover.

  16. Carry out Procedure after battery disconnection .

Charging the battery

🔧 Online öffnen

Charging the battery

⚠️ Important | An auxiliary starting system must not be used as this could damage the electronics of the car. | To start the car when the battery is discharged, use a booster battery or another car and jumper cables. |

  1. The battery can normally be charged with a conventional battery charger. Connect the red cable clip to the positive terminal (+) and the black cable clip to chassis ground.

  2. When boost-charging, the battery’s negative terminal must be disconnected. The charging current should not exceed 40 A when boost-charging. Max. charging time: 1.5 hours. ⚠️ Important Never boost-charge a battery if the voltage is below 12 V and the battery is more than 2 years old.

  3. If the battery is completely discharged due to the lights or some other source of consumption having been left on, a weak starting charge should be provided to initiate the chemical process in the battery. If this is the case, start charging with 3 A current (it must not exceed 5 A) for 24 hours or until the charging current has dropped to its lowest stable value. Gas starts to form in the battery at 14.4 V.

    If possible, a battery charger capable of delivering a pulsating charging current should be used.

Starter motor, B207

To remove

  1. Remove the battery cover.

  2. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

  3. Raise the car.

  4. Disconnect the starter motor connecting cables.

  5. Remove the starter motor retaining bolts.

  6. Lower the starter motor and lift it out.

To fit

  1. Lift the starter motor into place.

  2. Fit the starter motor retaining bolts. Tightening torque 47 Nm (35 lbf ft)

  3. Clean, apply Vaseline and connect the starter motor connecting cables.

  4. Lower the car to the floor.

  5. Connect the negative cable of the battery.

  6. Fit the battery cover.

  7. Reset the car; see Procedure after battery disconnection .

Starter motor, Z19

🔧 Online öffnen

Starter motor, Z19

To remove

  1. Remove the battery cover.

  2. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

  3. Remove the upper bolts from the starter motor.

  4. Remove the bracket from the coolant pipes and wiring harness.

  5. Raise the car.

  6. Remove the lower engine cover.

  7. Detach the starter motor connecting cables.

  8. Detach the ground cable from the lower stud on the starter motor.

  9. Remove the lower stud from the starter motor.

  10. Lower the starter motor and lift it out.

To fit

  1. Lift the starter motor into place and thread the lower stud.

  2. Lower the car.

  3. Fit the bracket to the coolant pipes and wiring harness.

  4. Fit the upper bolts to the starter motor. Tightening torque 24 Nm (18 lbf ft)

  5. Raise the car.

  6. Tighten the lower stud. Tightening torque 24 Nm (18 lbf ft)

  7. Connect the cables to the starter motor.

  8. Attach the ground cable to the lower stud.

  9. Fit the lower engine cover.

  10. Lower the car.

  11. Fit the negative battery cable.

  12. Fit the battery cover.

  13. Reset the car; see Procedures after battery disconnection .

Starter motor, replace, Z18XE

To remove
